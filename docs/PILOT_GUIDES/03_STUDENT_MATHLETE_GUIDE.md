# Guide 3 — Student Mathlete Guide

**Use this guide for:** signing in to the classroom competition, waiting in the correct Heat lobby, completing the Heat, and knowing when to ask the teacher for help.

> Your teacher gives you a private login card with a **username** and a **temporary PIN**. Keep the card private. Do not share either item with another student.[1]

## 1. Before the Heat starts

Bring the card your teacher gave you and the Heat link or code your teacher shares. You do not need to create your own account during the lesson. If you do not have a card, tell the teacher quietly before the Heat starts.

| You need | What it is | Keep it private? |
|---|---|---|
| Managed username | The username printed on your Mathathlone card | Yes |
| Temporary PIN | The six-digit number printed on the same card | Yes |
| Heat link or code | The competition your teacher asks you to join | Share only with classmates when the teacher says so |

Do not use another student’s card. The classroom Heat checks that you are actively enrolled in the exact class chosen by your teacher.[1]

## 2. Sign in

1. Open the Heat link your teacher shares. If you are not signed in, Mathathlone takes you to the login page and keeps the Heat destination for you.
2. Choose the **Mathlete** sign-in option.
3. Enter the username from your card. You may also use an ordinary email only if your teacher specifically provided one.
4. Enter your temporary PIN exactly as shown on the card.
5. Select the sign-in button.

If the sign-in works, Mathathlone returns you to the Heat you were opening. You should not need to search for it again.[1] [2]

| What you see | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Joining Heat…** | The system is checking your sign-in and class roster | Wait briefly. |
| **You’re in — Waiting for your teacher to start the Heat…** | You successfully joined the correct Heat | Stay on this page and wait. |
| **Session expired** | You were signed out | Select **Log in** and use your card again. |
| **This Heat doesn’t exist** | The code/link may be wrong | Check with your teacher; do not guess another code. |
| **This Heat has already started** | You were not in the lobby before the Heat began | Ask your teacher about the next or make-up Heat. |

## 3. While you wait in the lobby

Wait quietly after you see **You’re in**. The participant list lets the teacher confirm who has joined. Do not refresh repeatedly, close the tab, or begin work early. The teacher starts the Heat only after participants are present.

| Student responsibility | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Keep the correct Heat tab open | You remain ready when the teacher starts. |
| Keep your card private | It protects your account and competition identity. |
| Ask for help before the Heat starts | The teacher can reset a lost PIN or check your roster enrollment. |
| Do not sign in as someone else | The Heat is designed for the student enrolled in that class. |

## 4. Compete fairly

When the teacher starts the Heat, a short countdown appears and questions begin. Read each question carefully, enter your own answers, and follow the school’s usual assessment expectations. Use only the tools and accommodations that your teacher has approved before the Heat starts.

For the first pilot rehearsal, the purpose is to learn the process and improve the platform. A result is useful feedback; it is not a reason to compare yourself unfairly with students in another grade or a different class.

> Your grade cohort matters for fair comparison. Working on approved review material from a lower grade does not make you a lower-grade competitor; doing higher-level work can be recognized without changing who your grade-level peers are.[3]

## 5. After the Heat

Stay on the page while Mathathlone calculates results. This usually takes a few seconds. When the results screen appears, review it respectfully. Your teacher will explain how results are being used for the lesson or rehearsal.

| Situation | What to do |
|---|---|
| You finish early | Follow your teacher’s instructions; do not discuss questions with students who are still competing. |
| You see **Calculating results…** | Wait a short time. Tell the teacher if it does not change. |
| You cannot see your result | Tell the teacher your display name and Heat code; do not create another account. |
| You think there is an error | Tell the teacher after the Heat. Do not change answers or refresh repeatedly. |

## 6. If you need help

Tell your teacher privately if your card is missing, your username/PIN does not work, or you cannot join the Heat. The teacher can create a new PIN for your account. A new PIN replaces the old one immediately, so use the most recent card you receive.[1]

Do not ask classmates for their credentials and do not share yours. Do not post a username, PIN, Heat screenshot, or answers in a group chat or social-media account.
