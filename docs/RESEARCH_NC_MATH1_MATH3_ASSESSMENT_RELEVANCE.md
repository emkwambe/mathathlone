# Research Notes — NC Math 1 and NC Math 3 Assessment Relevance

**Retrieved:** 2026-09-02
**Purpose:** Support grant-transition and pilot-course planning. These notes do not authorize use of NCDPI test items in Mathathlone.

## Official findings

The North Carolina Department of Public Instruction (NCDPI) states that NC Math 1 and NC Math 3 End-of-Course (EOC) tests measure proficiency on the North Carolina Standard Course of Study for Mathematics. NCDPI’s current EOC specifications state that operational EOC results are used as at least 20% of the final course grade under State Board policy TEST-003. The specifications describe both courses as 60-item tests: 50 operational and 10 field-test items. NC Math 1 has calculator-inactive and calculator-active sections; NC Math 3 is calculator-active only. The specifications state that EOC items span Number and Quantity/Algebra, Functions, Geometry, and Statistics/Probability and include DOK 1, 2, and 3 distributions.

NCDPI states that NC Check-Ins 2.0 for NC Math 1 and NC Math 3 are interim assessments aligned to course standards. Their stated primary purpose is immediate in-depth action-data and formative use on selected standards. NCDPI explicitly says that it lacks validity evidence to support using Check-In results to predict EOC performance. The specifications describe flexible local administration windows and note that schools may not have taught every standard assessed in a Check-In; untaught content should be used as pre-test data.

NCDPI’s EOC page states that released materials are copyrighted, must not be used for personal or financial gain, and cannot be uploaded into third-party applications. Mathathlone must therefore use its own original procedural generators and standard mappings, not copied/rehosted NCDPI released items.
