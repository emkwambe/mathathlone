# Mathathlone — DEMO Staff Role and Credential Directory

**Use:** Three-school model-pilot rehearsal. Page 1 defines the District Coordinator, School Coordinator, and Teacher roles. Page 2 groups every DEMO account by district or school. It contains **no passwords**; write temporary passwords privately in the final column only.

> **Authority rule:** Give each person only the role and organization scope needed. No coordinator gains platform-wide authority, and no teacher owns another teacher’s class.[1]

## Page 1 of 2 — Role descriptions and boundaries

| Role | Scope | Current pilot responsibility | Not permitted | Intended production role |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **District Coordinator** | One district; home school optional. | Confirms participating schools/coordinators, coordinates the agreed window outside the application, collects readiness feedback, and escalates scope/platform issues. | Create schools/districts; assign staff; access another district; create classes; manage student rosters/PINs. | Coordinate district participation, approved blueprint windows, and later district aggregate reporting. |
| **School Coordinator** | One school only. | Confirms teachers are at the correct school, verifies re-login, supports non-sensitive access questions, and records local operational feedback. | Assign staff; manage another school; create teacher-owned classes; distribute/reset student PINs; alter a teacher’s class or Heat. | Coordinate school readiness and later school-level reporting while teachers retain classroom ownership. |
| **Teacher** | Exactly one school; own classes only. | Creates classes and rosters; issues managed Mathlete cards; resets a student PIN when needed; creates student-safe practice and class-bound Heats; reviews own results. | Manage another teacher’s class; assign staff; create schools; give students a practice answer key; admit a non-rostered Mathlete. | Operate classroom preparation, competition, and feedback at the assigned school. |

### Required operating sequence

| Step | Owner | Action | Pass condition |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Platform Owner | Assign each existing staff account to the intended district or school. | Pilot console shows the correct active scope. |
| 2 | New staff member | Sign out, close the Mathathlone tab, sign in again, and open `/dashboard`. | Expected dashboard appears. |
| 3 | Teacher | Create the class, import approved minimal student data, and keep cards private. | Teacher sees only their own rostered class. |
| 4 | Teacher | Announce concepts, issue a Practice Review, return to Builder, then create a class-bound Heat. | Practice names skills; Heat uses new generated instances.[2] |

**Current boundary:** Teacher class/roster/worksheet/Heat work is available now. Dedicated coordinator dashboards, flexible cross-school comparison, scheduled simultaneous events, formal team scoring, and school/district reports remain follow-on releases. When production is ready, create new real staff accounts; never rename a `demo.*@mathathlone.test` account into a real person.
